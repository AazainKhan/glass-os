---
title: What is Glass
description: An open-source, Apple TV-style home screen for Fire TV and Android TV. A plain-text overview.
---

Glass is a launcher: the screen you see when you press Home. It takes its look and motion from Apple TV (frosted glass panels, soft focus lift, a top row of apps, folders, a featured shelf, Aerial screensaver videos) and runs on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. It is *Apple TV-style*, not a copy, and it is not affiliated with Apple, Amazon or Google.

It is written for weak hardware first. The reference device is a Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd gen), and the project measures frame times on it before it ships a visual change.

## What we are honest about

- **Tested on one device.** Other Fire TV, Android TV and Google TV devices should work, but nobody has verified that yet.
- **A launcher cannot be everything on Fire OS.** Fire's key policy keeps the Home button for itself, so without root Glass takes over by bouncing Amazon's launcher out of the way. The [no-root page](/glass-os/no-root/) says exactly what you get.
- **The deeper tiers are only for people who want them.** Glass Pro and Glass OS are not built or released yet.
