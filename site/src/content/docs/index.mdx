## What Glass is

Glass is a launcher: the screen you see when you press Home. It takes its look and motion from Apple TV (frosted glass panels, soft focus lift, a top row of apps, folders, a featured shelf, Aerial screensaver videos) and runs on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. It is *Apple TV-style*, not a copy, and it is not affiliated with Apple, Amazon or Google.

It is written for weak hardware first. The reference device is a Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd gen), and the project measures frame times on it before it ships a visual change.

## What we are honest about

- **Tested on one device.** The Fire TV Stick 4K 2nd gen is the only hardware Glass has been checked on. Other Fire TV, Android TV and Google TV devices should work, but nobody has verified that yet.
- **A launcher cannot be everything on Fire OS.** Fire's key policy keeps the Home button for itself, so without root Glass takes over by bouncing Amazon's launcher out of the way, not by intercepting the button. The [no-root page](/glass-os/no-root/) says exactly what you get and what you do not.
- **The deeper tiers are only for people who want them.** Glass Pro and Glass OS are for tinkerers with a rooted or unlocked stick. They are not built or released yet, and the pages say so.
