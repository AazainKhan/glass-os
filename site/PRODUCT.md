# Product

## Register
Persuade (landing and tier pages). Docs section is Read.

## What it is
Glass: an open-source, Apple TV-style launcher (home screen) for Fire TV and Android TV, with optional deeper tiers (Glass Pro Magisk module, Glass OS image; both not released). Site doubles as docs and agent-readable runbook (llms.txt).

## Audience
Fire TV Stick owners who dislike Amazon's ad-heavy home screen; tinkerers comfortable with adb/Magisk; agents. Seen on a laptop or phone, installing onto a TV.

## Voice
Friendly, honest, plain. "Apple TV-style", never "tvOS" or "1:1". Anything unbuilt says "coming soon". No invented claims.

## Constraints
Starlight docs and llms.txt stay. No Apple trademarks, no SF Pro. Reduced-motion respected. Fast, minimal JS. Mobile-good. Hero screenshot slot src/assets/hero.png (user supplies later).

## Assumptions
Init interview not held (no answer channel); inferred from the coordinator's brief.
